## Summary
Analyses the rank reversal phenomenon in TOPSIS and proposes modifications to the ideal and negative-ideal solution definitions to improve ranking stability. Provides a theoretical and numerical treatment of when and why rank reversal occurs.

## Key Arguments
- Rank reversal in TOPSIS occurs when the ideal or negative-ideal solution changes upon addition or removal of alternatives
- Fixing the reference points addresses the root cause of instability
- Modified TOPSIS produces more reliable rankings for practical decision support
- The problem is non-trivial and cannot be dismissed as an edge case

## Key Concepts
- Rank reversal
- Ideal solution stability
- TOPSIS modification
- MCDM reliability
