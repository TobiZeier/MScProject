## Summary
Systematic literature review examining strategies, processes, factors, and tools for TD prioritisation. Finds that the field lacks a unified, evidence-based prioritisation approach and that most existing methods are either ad hoc or tool-dependent.

## Key Arguments
- No consensus prioritisation strategy exists across the TD literature
- Most prioritisation approaches rely on a single criterion (usually estimated remediation cost or severity rating)
- Multi-criteria approaches are underrepresented despite the inherently multi-dimensional nature of TD
- Business impact is rarely formally incorporated into prioritisation decisions

## Key Concepts
- TD prioritisation strategies
- Single-criterion vs multi-criteria prioritisation
- Business impact linkage
- Systematic literature review

## Relevance to Project
Anchor paper for section 2.6.2 (The Prioritisation Gap). Establishes the state of the art against which my TOPSIS-based framework is positioned as a contribution.
