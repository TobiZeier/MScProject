## Summary
Empirical study of TD remediation in outsourced enterprise systems maintenance contracts. Finds that TD accumulation significantly reduces IT architecture reliability and that remediation under outsourcing conditions is structurally constrained by contract incentives.

## Key Arguments
- TD accumulation measurably decreases the reliability of IT architecture
- Outsourcing arrangements create perverse incentives that accelerate TD accumulation and inhibit remediation
- Remediation costs escalate non-linearly as TD compounds over time
- Organisations that fail to manage TD get trapped in complex webs of systems, losing agility

## Key Concepts
- TD remediation economics
- Outsourced maintenance
- IT reliability degradation
- Contract incentive misalignment

## Relevance to Project
Section 2.7.2 (Enterprise Systems and the Outsourcing Context). Provides empirical grounding for the claim that unmanaged TD directly reduces IT reliability, a core assumption of the proposed framework.
