## Summary
Systematic literature review of 72 studies on software security patch management. Identifies 14 socio-technical challenges, 18 solution approaches, and maps gaps in the literature. One of the anchor papers for citation snowballing.

## Key Arguments
- Patch management is a complex socio-technical process involving multiple stakeholders and interdependent decisions
- 14 distinct challenges span technical, organisational, and process dimensions
- No unified framework addresses the full patch management lifecycle
- SLA constraints and operational risk are underrepresented in existing solutions

## Key Concepts
- Patch management lifecycle
- Socio-technical challenges
- Vulnerability remediation
- SLA-driven patching

## Relevance to Project
Section 2.7.4 (Security Debt and Patch Management) and as an anchor paper for snowballing. Patch lag is one of my framework's TD indicators, this paper provides the systematic evidence base for its inclusion.
