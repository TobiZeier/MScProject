## Summary
Systematic mapping study of how machine learning and AI techniques are being applied to TDM tasks including identification, measurement, prioritisation, and monitoring.

## Key Arguments
- ML is increasingly applied to TD identification (code smell detection, smell prediction)
- Intelligent techniques for prioritisation are rare, most approaches remain rule-based or manual
- Combining ML with MCDM frameworks is identified as a high-potential future direction
- Data availability and interpretability are key challenges for ML in TDM

## Key Concepts
- ML for TD identification
- Intelligent TDM
- Explainability and interpretability
- MCDM as complementary approach

## Relevance to Project
Supports the use of structured quantitative methods (TOPSIS) over ML for interpretability in a regulated context. Maybe section 2.8.3 (Transferability Critique and Ethical Dimensions)
