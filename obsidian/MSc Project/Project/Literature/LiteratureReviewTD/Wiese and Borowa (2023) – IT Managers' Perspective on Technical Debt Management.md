## Summary
Qualitative study based on interviews with IT managers examining how they perceive, manage, and prioritise technical debt in practice. Reveals a large gap between academic TDM models and real-world practice.

## Key Arguments
- IT managers acknowledge TD is a major problem but lack structured tools to manage it
- Prioritisation decisions are driven by business pressure, not technical metrics
- Visibility of TD across the organisation is poor
- There is a strong demand for decision-support tools that translate technical TD metrics into business impact language

## Key Concepts
- Managerial perspective on TD
- Business-driven prioritisation
- TD visibility and communication

## Relevance to Project
Relevant to section 2.6 (Measurement and Prioritisation Frameworks). Strongly supports the practical motivation for a decision-support artefact, hence, section 2.9.4 (Transition to Research Questions). Confirms that structured MCDM-based prioritisation would address a real managerial need. Cited in Literature Review Themes 1 and 4.
