## Summary
Action research study proposing and evaluating a five-step workshop approach for establishing TD management practices in organisations that currently have none. Demonstrates that structured governance interventions can create shared awareness and initiate management routines where previously absent.

## Key Arguments
- Many organisations lack any structured TD management practice despite awareness of the problem
- A facilitated workshop approach can bootstrap TD governance from scratch
- Shared vocabulary and cross-team awareness are prerequisites for effective management
- TD management requires organisational commitment, not just tooling

## Key Concepts
- TD governance bootstrapping
- Workshop-based intervention
- Organisational TD awareness
- Action research

## Relevance to Project
Supports section 2.1 (Context): evidence that TD management is absent in practice. Also relevant to section 2.5.3 (Expanding the Perimeter) as a governance-oriented management framework.
