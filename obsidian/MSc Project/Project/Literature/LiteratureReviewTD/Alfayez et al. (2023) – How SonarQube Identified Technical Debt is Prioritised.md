## Summary
Exploratory case study examining how developers actually prioritise TD items identified by SonarQube. Finds that prioritisation is largely informal and relies on developer intuition rather than structured methods.

## Key Arguments
- SonarQube flags large numbers of TD items but gives little guidance on what to fix first
- Developers use personal judgement, effort estimates, and business impact as informal criteria
- No systematic MCDM or quantitative prioritisation is applied
- Prioritisation is inconsistent across teams

## Key Concepts
- TD prioritisation gap
- Informal vs structured prioritisation
- Code smell severity

## Relevance to Project
Addresses section 2.6.2 (The Prioritisation Gap). Directly motivates the need for a structured, quantitative TOPSIS-based framework. Cited in Literature Review Theme 3 and 2.9 (Synthesis: Converging Evidence and the Research Gap).
