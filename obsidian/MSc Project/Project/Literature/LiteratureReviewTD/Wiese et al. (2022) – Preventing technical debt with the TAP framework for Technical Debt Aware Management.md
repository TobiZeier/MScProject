## Summary
Industrial case study proposing the TAP framework (Technical debt Aware Project management) for integrating technical debt management directly into project management processes. Evaluated in an IT unit of a publishing company using ticket statistics and surveys of developers and managers. The framework introduces explicit backlog categories including TD tickets to ensure intentional debt decisions and scheduled repayment. Results show increased TD awareness, more deliberate decision making, improved communication between stakeholders, and more timely repayment of debt. 

## Key Arguments
- Technical debt prevention should be prioritised over repayment because later refactoring is significantly more expensive 
- TD often emerges from tight deadlines and informal decision making rather than deliberate technical strategy 
- Integrating TD management into project management processes improves visibility and accountability
- Explicit TD tickets make debt incurrence intentional and trackable
- Distinguishing between intentional and unintentional TD enables different management strategies
- Organisational awareness and communication are central to effective TD management

## Key Concepts
- TAP framework (Technical debt Aware Project management)
- TD prevention vs TD repayment
- Intentional vs unintentional technical debt
- TD backlog tickets
- Technical debt awareness
- Integration of TD management into project management
