## Summary
Argues that technical debt should be understood and measured at the systems level, not just the code level. Proposes a conceptual extension of TD to encompass infrastructure, integration, and operational dimensions.

## Key Arguments
- Code-centric TD frameworks miss a large portion of real-world debt in complex systems
- Systems-level TD includes infrastructure debt, integration debt, and operational process debt
- TD at the system level manifests in reliability degradation, not just code quality metrics
- Measurement needs to incorporate operational and architectural data sources

## Key Concepts
- Systems-level TD
- Infrastructure debt
- Operational TD manifestations

## Relevance to Project
Theoretical foundation for extending TD measurement to the service level. Can be used in section 2.6.1 (The Dominance of Static Analysis and Its Limits). Cited in Introduction and Literature Review Theme 1.
