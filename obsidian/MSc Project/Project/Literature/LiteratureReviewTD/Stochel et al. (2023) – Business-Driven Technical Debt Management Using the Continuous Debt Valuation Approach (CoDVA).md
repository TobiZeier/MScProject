## Summary
Proposes CoDVA, a continuous TD valuation approach that links TD items to business value impact over time. Aimed at providing managers with an ongoing, business-contextualised view of TD rather than a static snapshot.

## Key Arguments
- TD must be valued in terms of its business impact, not just remediation cost
- Static TD assessments are insufficient, valuation must be continuous to reflect changing business context
- Business-driven prioritisation produces different rankings than cost-driven or severity-driven approaches
- CoDVA integrates TD management into regular business governance cycles

## Key Concepts
- Continuous TD valuation
- Business-value linkage
- CoDVA methodology
- TD governance integration

## Relevance to Project
Section 2.6 (Measurement and Prioritisation Frameworks). Supports the argument that TD prioritisation must be linked to business outcomes, consistent with my use of operational service metrics as outcome proxies.
