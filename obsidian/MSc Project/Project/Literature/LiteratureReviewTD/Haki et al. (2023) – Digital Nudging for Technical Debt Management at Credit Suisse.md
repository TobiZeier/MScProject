## Summary
Case study of TD management practice at Credit Suisse, examining how digital nudging mechanisms were used to encourage developers to address TD items. One of the very few empirical TD studies conducted in a large regulated financial services institution.

## Key Arguments
- In large financial institutions, developer motivation to address TD is low without explicit incentive mechanisms
- Digital nudges (automated visibility of TD items in development workflows) improve TD awareness and resolution rates
- Governance and cultural factors are as important as tooling in regulated environments
- TD accumulation in financial IT is structurally different from software product companies

## Key Concepts
- Digital nudging
- TD governance in financial services
- Developer behaviour and incentives
- Regulated enterprise IT
