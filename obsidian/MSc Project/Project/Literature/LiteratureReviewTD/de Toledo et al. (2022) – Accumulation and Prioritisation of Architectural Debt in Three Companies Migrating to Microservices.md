## Summary
Multiple exploratory case study across three large companies migrating to microservices. Interviews 47 practitioners to identify which microservice-specific ATDs occur, which are most severe, and which are most difficult to resolve. Proposes a risk-assessment-based prioritisation approach.

## Key Arguments
- Microservice-specific ATDs accumulate during migration and follow recognisable patterns
- Practitioners identify severity and difficulty-to-resolve as the two primary prioritisation dimensions
- Risk assessment provides a practical basis for ATD prioritisation in migration contexts
- Poor API design and compatibility maintenance are among the most common and costly ATDs

## Key Concepts
- Microservice-specific ATD
- Migration-phase debt accumulation
- Risk-based prioritisation
- Practitioner perspectives

## Relevance to Project
Section 2.6.2 (The Prioritisation Gap) and section 2.5.3 (Expanding the Conceptual Perimeter). Supports the argument that domain-specific, multi-factor prioritisation is necessary and that generic approaches are insufficient.
