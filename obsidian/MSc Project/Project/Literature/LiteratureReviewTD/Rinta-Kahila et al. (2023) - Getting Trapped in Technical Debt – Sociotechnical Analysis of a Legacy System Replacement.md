## Summary
In-depth socio-technical case study of a failed legacy system replacement. Analyses how accumulated TD, organisational inertia, and vendor lock-in combined to trap the organisation in an increasingly costly debt cycle.

## Key Arguments
- Legacy system TD accumulates through a combination of technical and organisational factors
- Replacement attempts often fail because the full scope of TD is not understood upfront
- TD creates path dependency, the longer it is deferred, the harder replacement becomes
- Organisational and management debt are often more constraining than code-level debt

## Key Concepts
- Legacy system trap
- Socio-technical TD
- Path dependency
- Vendor lock-in

## Relevance to Project
Illustrates the real-world consequences of unmanaged TD in enterprise IT. Relevant to section 2.7.2 (Enterprise Systems and the Outsourcing Context) and Literature Review Theme 1 (TD in regulated/enterprise environments) and motivation for the research.
