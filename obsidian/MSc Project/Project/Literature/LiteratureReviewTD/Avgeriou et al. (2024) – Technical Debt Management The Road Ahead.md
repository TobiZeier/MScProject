## Summary
Position paper by leading TD researchers examining a decade of research and practice in TD management. Identifies persistent gaps between available tooling and consistent management practice and articulates a five-year vision for the field. Describes TD as the "silent killer" of software projects.

## Key Arguments
- A decade of TD research has not translated into consistent management practice in industry
- Significant gaps remain in tooling applicability, prioritisation support, and business-value linkage
- TD management must be integrated into day-to-day engineering practice rather than treated as a separate activity
- Research has focused heavily on identification and measurement while neglecting prioritisation and repayment

## Key Concepts
- TD management maturity
- Research-practice gap
- TD as silent killer
- Five-year research vision

## Relevance to Project
Directly addresses section 2.6.2 (The Prioritisation Gap), research shows TD is not managed properly. Also, section 2.1 (Context of the Topic) supports the claim that TD management is widely discussed yet structurally unresolved. Also useful in section 2.9 (Synthesis) as evidence of the field's self-acknowledged limitations.
