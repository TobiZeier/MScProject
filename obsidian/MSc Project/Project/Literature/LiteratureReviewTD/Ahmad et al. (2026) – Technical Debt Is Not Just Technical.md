## Summary
Qualitative case study across four international companies involving 24 expert interviews. Identifies five non-technical dimensions of TD in large-scale agile contexts: social dynamics, process, people, documentation, and requirements. Concludes that lack of communication and collaboration are the primary drivers. 

## Key Arguments
- TD in large organisations is fundamentally rooted in communication and collaboration failures, not purely technical decisions
- Five non-technical TD aspects each manifest distinct indicators in agile contexts
- Culture of not developing rules, protocols, or guidelines is a root cause of non-technical debt accumulation
- Technical and non-technical debt are mutually reinforcing 

## Key Concepts
- Non-technical debt
- Social debt
- Large-scale agile
- Organisational TD dimensions

## Relevance to Project
Supports section 2.5.3 (Expanding the Perimeter) and Section 2.9.1 (Cross-Pillar Convergence). Challenges code-centric TD frameworks and reinforces the need for service-level measurement that captures organisational and operational dimensions of debt.
