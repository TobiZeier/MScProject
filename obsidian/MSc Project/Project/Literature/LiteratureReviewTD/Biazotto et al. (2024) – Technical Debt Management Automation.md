## Summary
Systematic review of the state of automation in TDM. Catalogues tools, techniques, and automation levels across the TDM lifecycle (identification, measurement, prioritisation, remediation, monitoring).

## Key Arguments
- Automation is mature for TD identification and measurement but weak for prioritisation and remediation
- Most automated tools focus on code-level debt
- Decision support for prioritisation remains largely manual
- Future directions include ML-based prioritisation and multi-criteria approaches

## Key Concepts
- TDM lifecycle (identify → measure → prioritise → remediate → monitor)
- Automation gaps
- ML and MCDM as future directions

## Relevance to Project
Confirms the gap in automated prioritisation in section 2.6.2 (The Prioritisation Gap) and validates MCDM/TOPSIS as a promising direction. Cited in Literature Review Theme 3.
